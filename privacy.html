Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026.

What the app accesses

With your permission, the app uses the Google Sheets API (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets) to read and write the one spreadsheet you choose in its settings. It uses this access only to keep that spreadsheet and the app in sync.

Where your data goes

Your data is stored only on your phone and in your own Google spreadsheet. The app has no server. It does not collect, share, sell or transfer your data to anyone, and it contains no analytics or advertising.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app deletes its local data.
